### Indiana Pacers Revolutionize Fan Experience with Azure Functions: A STAR Story

#### **Situation:**
The Pacers Sports & Entertainment (PS&E) team was driven to enhance accessibility within their live basketball games for fans who are deaf, hard of hearing, or non-English speakers. Traditional speech-to-text solutions were too slow and imprecise to capture the fast-paced, dynamic commentary of live games. Without a custom solution, many fans couldn't fully enjoy the immersive basketball experience at Gainbridge Fieldhouse.

#### **Task:**
The team set out to create a first-of-its-kind real-time captioning system. The solution needed to process speech instantly, recognize game-specific vocabulary (like player names and basketball slang), and display captions on both arena screens and mobile apps—all without noticeable delays. 

#### **Action:**
By leveraging **Azure Functions**—Microsoft's serverless compute service—alongside other Azure tools like Azure AI Foundry and Azure SignalR Service, the team implemented an **event-driven architecture**. Through Azure Functions:
- Audio commentary was processed in real time and seamlessly synchronized into live text on mobile apps and arena screens.
- The captions were kept in sync across all platforms without delays by routing them through a **single, consistent source**.
- Microsoft Azure AI Speech tools were used to train custom models with curated game audio and player data, increasing transcription accuracy vastly.

Azure Functions ensured smooth workflow integration with PS&E’s existing digital infrastructure, allowing for rapid deployment without disrupting operations. Additionally, the modular, serverless nature of Azure Functions provided a scalable and future-ready solution for incorporating more languages and features, such as sentiment tracking and personalization.

#### **Result:**
The result was a game-changing, **AI-powered captioning system** with a remarkable **1.14% error rate**. This innovative solution:
1. Empowered fans with accessibility needs, delivering instant captions both at the game and on their devices.
2. Reduced model training time by **99.97%**, from five days to just two minutes.
3. Transformed attendees' experiences, from live games to mobile apps.
 
The Pacers didn’t just make live games more inclusive—they also redefined what’s possible in sports entertainment with Azure Functions and AI. The success of the project positioned PS&E as pioneers in using AI to create meaningful fan engagement at massive scales.
